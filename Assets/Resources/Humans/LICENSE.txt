The human body data in this folder (topology.bytes and the body shape .bytes files) was converted from the
MakeHuman base mesh, macro targets and default skin weights:
https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman (makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj, data/targets/macrodetails,
data/rigs/default_weights.mhw).

These MakeHuman assets are released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
You may use, modify and sell games containing them without permission or attribution.

To regenerate or add body shapes, run Tools/makehuman_export.py (see the comments at the top of that file).
